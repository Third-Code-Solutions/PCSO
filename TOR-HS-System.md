# TERMS OF REFERENCE

## PROCUREMENT OF HUMAN RESOURCE SYSTEM (HRS)

## I. BACKGROUND

The Human Resource (HR) System will be a web-based platform designed to automate the processing of transactions within the Human Resources Department (HRD). This initiative is part of the PCSO Information Systems Strategic Plan (ISSP), which aims to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of HR operations across both the agency's head office and its branches.

The procurement process for the HR System will adhere to competitive bidding procedures in accordance with Republic Act No. 12009, also known as the New Government Procurement Act, and its Implementing Rules and Regulations. This is to guarantee a procurement process that upholds the principles of transparency, accountability, and fairness.

To support PCSO's commitment to streamlined operations, digital transformation, and responsive public service, the Human Resource System (HRS) shall serve as a critical tool in modernizing personnel administration, improving data accuracy, and strengthening compliance with civil service standards. The system is envisioned to centralize and automate key HR functions, ranging from recruitment and timekeeping to payroll and performance evaluation, thereby promoting accountability, operational efficiency, and data-driven decision-making across the organization.

## II. OBJECTIVES

The HRS aims to improve PCSO's human resource processes, including employee records, compensation and benefits, learning and development, and performance management, among others. Through the adoption of new technologies, the PCSO seeks to enhance operational efficiency and effectiveness. The HRS will integrate all HR functions across the organization into a unified platform, ensuring compliance with the policies, rules, and regulations of the PCSO, the Civil Service Commission (CSC), the Governance Commission for GOCCs (GCG), and other relevant government agencies.

## III. SCOPE AND REQUIREMENTS

The provider must design, develop, customize, test, deliver, and install the HRS, perform data migration, provide the equipment, tools and software necessary for the system's implementation; prepare necessary documentation for the entire project and ensure that existing government rules and regulations are considered.

The HRS shall cover the following:

**HR SYSTEM MAJOR MODULES**

- File Maintenance (FM)
- Recruitment and Promotion Module (RPM)
- Personnel Information Management Module (PIMM)
- Leave Administration Module (LAM)
- Time and Attendance Module (TAM)
- Learning and Development Module (LDM)
- Performance Management Module (PMM)
- HR Portal Module (HRPM)
- Payroll Module (PM)
- Employee Portal Module (EPM)
- Employees Welfare and Benefits Module (EWBM)
